## Reviews fail when they demand a perfect week

Many weekly review systems quietly assume that the week unfolded exactly as planned. Real weeks do not behave that way. People miss tasks, switch priorities, and carry unresolved decisions longer than they wanted to.

When the ritual ignores that reality, the review becomes a performance instead of a tool.

## The shape we prefer

Our review pattern is intentionally small:

1. What moved?
2. What stalled?
3. What still matters next week?

That framing keeps the ritual honest. It does not punish you for having a messy week. It asks for a clear handoff from one week to the next.

## What the product should do

Software can either help this ritual or make it heavier. We want Orgo to do three quiet jobs during review:

- surface unfinished work with context attached
- make it easy to close loops that no longer matter
- leave a short written trace of what changed

The goal is not a beautiful archive for its own sake. The goal is to start Monday without needing to rebuild your own memory from scratch.
