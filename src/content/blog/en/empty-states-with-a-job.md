## Blank does not mean neutral

The first empty state in a product sets emotional tone. It can make someone feel invited, judged, lost, or quietly guided. That is why empty states deserve more care than a generic illustration and an optimistic sentence.

## The jobs we assign to them

An empty state should do at least one useful thing:

- explain what belongs here
- suggest the smallest meaningful next action
- reassure the user that nothing is broken

If it cannot do one of those jobs, it is probably just decoration.

## Why restraint helps

We prefer spare empty states because people encounter them during moments of uncertainty. The interface should help them orient quickly, not ask them to parse a mini marketing panel. The right amount of guidance is often a short sentence, a helpful example, and one clear action.

That is enough to turn a blank surface into a beginning.
