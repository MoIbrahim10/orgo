## Calm is a product discipline

It is easy to mistake calmness for an aesthetic preference. In reality, it is a set of constraints. If you want a tool to feel calm, you must decide what does _not_ deserve foreground treatment.

## The principles we return to

We keep a short set of checks nearby:

- does this feature clarify the next decision?
- does it preserve context rather than create more of it?
- does it ask for attention proportionate to its importance?

If the answer fails those checks, the feature may still be useful, but it probably needs a different shape.

## Why principles matter

Without principles, calmness disappears under pressure. Deadlines arrive, features pile up, and every new thing argues for visibility. Principles are what let a team stay coherent when taste alone is not enough.

For us, they are also editorial tools. They help decide what deserves a post, how much explanation a change needs, and when silence is more respectful than noise.
