## One page can carry more than most dashboards

When a team feels scattered, the instinct is often to add another board or reporting surface. We usually get better results from something smaller: a weekly brief that fits on one page and answers the obvious questions before anyone has to ask them.

## What belongs in the brief

The version we like includes:

- the handful of priorities that truly shape the week
- key decisions already made
- open questions that could block momentum

If the brief becomes longer than that, it stops being a briefing and starts becoming a document archive.

## Why it works

The weekly brief creates a shared reference point. It helps people understand the week at a glance and gives loose notes somewhere to resolve into a clearer story. Inside Orgo, we think of this pattern as a bridge between daily execution and longer editorial memory.
