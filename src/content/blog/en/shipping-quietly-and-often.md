## Loud launches are not the only sign of progress

There is a certain kind of product culture that equates volume with momentum. Big announcements, large batches of change, constant ceremony. That can work for some teams. For Orgo, it would work against the feeling we are trying to create.

## Our preferred rhythm

We like smaller releases with clear intent:

- improve one part of the weekly flow
- explain the change in plain language
- give the team enough time to notice second-order effects

This keeps the product from feeling volatile. It also keeps the journal useful, because each entry can stay focused on a real decision instead of turning into a rushed grab bag.

## The long-term effect

A steady rhythm builds trust. Users do not need to brace for sweeping change every time they open the product. Internally, the team gets a cleaner record of what changed and why. The product becomes easier to evolve because the pace itself leaves room for attention.
