## Most meeting fatigue happens afterward

The exhausting part is not always the meeting itself. It is the vague residue it leaves behind: half-written notes, unclear ownership, and follow-up work that never becomes real enough to schedule.

## The checklist we rely on

After important meetings, we run a short cleanup pass:

- capture the key decision in one sentence
- separate commitments from general discussion
- assign the next owner and timing before context cools off

This takes minutes, but it radically improves recall later.

## Why tools should support the ritual

If the product makes cleanup cumbersome, people skip it. If the product makes it natural to turn a note into a decision and a decision into next action, the ritual becomes easier to keep.

That is the standard we use when designing meeting-adjacent workflows in Orgo: fewer loose ends, less reconstruction, more clarity when the week is already moving on.
