## Design Context

### Users
The blog serves a mixed audience. Readers are likely switching between technical and non-technical topics, and they want article pages that are expressive but still easy to scan for the core narrative.

### Brand Personality
The preferred tone blends editorial, minimal, calm, premium, experimental, playful, and bold qualities. The design direction should feel intentional and crafted, not template-like.

### Aesthetic Direction
Support both light and dark reading contexts without forcing one mode. The article detail page should explore varied compositions while keeping content clarity, with each variant having a distinct structure and visual voice.

### Design Principles
1. Keep the article content readable first, then layer in personality.
2. Separate visual directions should feel genuinely different, not small style tweaks.
3. Expose clear controls for picking a final direction and mixing successful parts.
4. Preserve core conversion content, especially related posts and subscribe sections.
5. Maintain responsive behavior and avoid fragile layout tricks.
